What it accepts

      
Enterprise knowledge doesn't arrive as clean text. It arrives as whatever format the work actually happens in.

    

    

      
Multi-page PDFs & scans
Forms, reports, and contracts — typed or scanned.

      
Spreadsheets with formulas
Cell relationships and formulas kept intact, not flattened to CSV.

      
Diagrams & flowcharts
Shapes, connectors, and labels read as a graph, not a caption.

      
Mixed-language & handwritten content
Tagged and confidence-scored per region, wherever it appears.

      
Tables & multi-column layouts
Reading order and cell structure preserved.

      
Your own artifact type
New adapters register into the same schema — no rearchitecture.

What comes out

      
One extraction, shaped four ways — the same four views whether the source was a page, a sheet, or a diagram.

    

    

      
textPlain text
Flattened, reading-order-correct prose for search indexing.

      
structuredStructured fields
Typed nodes — cells, regions, diagram shapes — with position and relationships.

      
jsonAudit JSON
Full record with confidence and provenance, for compliance review.

      
tableTable export
Flattened rows — line items, cell formulas, or an edge list — for analytics.

    

  


  

    

      
Why it's better than flattening

      
The claim only means something if it's measured — a downstream task run against a naive flatten-to-text baseline versus the structured pipeline, on the same held-out artifacts.

    

    

      
Documents — field lookup accuracy

      

        
61% · flattened text

        
89% · structured

      

    

    

      
Spreadsheets — formula & total questions

      

        
34% · flattened text

        
92% · structured

      

    

    

      
Diagrams — "what connects to what" questions

      

        
28% · flattened text

        
81% · structured

      

    

    
Illustrative targets from the evaluation methodology, not measured results — the real numbers come from running this harness against a held-out sample once each adapter is built.
